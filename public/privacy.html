What the app does with your Google data

The app uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API v3 only for the owner's own YouTube channels, in order to:

  • read and update video, playlist, caption, comment and live broadcast data;
  • upload videos and schedule when they are published;
  • set video thumbnails;
  • read, post and moderate live chat messages;
  • post and moderate comments;
  • manage channel branding (banner, description, keywords, watermark, sections).

It requests these Google permissions: youtube.force-ssl (manage your YouTube account) and youtube.upload (upload videos).

Where your data goes

All processing happens in your browser. The app talks directly to Google's APIs and does not send your data to any other server. Your data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Access tokens are kept in browser memory and session storage only, and are discarded when you close the tab or sign out. App settings (such as chat commands, presets and upload defaults) are stored in your browser's local storage, and you can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
